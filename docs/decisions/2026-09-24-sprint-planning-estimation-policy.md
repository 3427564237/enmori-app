## Prospective policy

From Sprint 3 onward, Sprint planning has no fixed per-member or team hour ceiling. A task-hour estimate and Story Points are not required for refinement, Sprint selection, or `Ready`; the team does not assign either as a planning field. The prior `20h/member`, `100h/team`, `1/2/3/5/8` Point scale, and automatic split above 8 Points no longer govern new work.

Members record actual hours only after work has occurred, for retrospective or course evidence. Actual hours are not converted into a capacity limit, estimate, Story Points, or a `Ready`/`Done` gate. Do not infer planned effort from those records.

LI still assigns owners and Sprint scope. Members surface actual availability, workload, dependencies, and feasibility concerns; LI resolves priority and scope. Keep work reasonably distributed across suitable owners without artificial equality or invented tasks. A large or uneven assignment needs a practical explanation, not a numeric-hours exception.

`Ready` still requires an authoritative source, testable acceptance criteria, a clear scope boundary, resolution of affected blockers, known dependencies, a PM-assigned owner, a different reviewer, and applicable platform/device gates. `Done` still requires the existing implementation, self-test, independent review, integration/platform evidence, merge, and LI acceptance chain. A removed estimate never waives product, privacy, security, accessibility, review, or evidence obligations. Report unresolved affected behavior truthfully while independent approved work continues.

## Supersession and limits

This changes the planning rules above permanently for Sprint 3 and later Sprints. It does not rewrite the historical Sprint 1 staffing approvals, Sprint 2's approved 104h exception, previously accepted Point values, or recorded actual work. Those records retain their original scope and meaning.

This process decision does not approve any Sprint 3 product behavior, the five Q04-D `Prototype Evidence Only` routes, a Backend/API/data-model change, `Ready` or `Done` status, or a GitHub/Notion write. Sprint 3 feature assignments and coverage targets are described separately in the [handoff](../../ASTRA_HANDOFF_S2_VISUAL_GAP_PREP_20260923.md) and remain subject to their governing product decisions and observed evidence.
