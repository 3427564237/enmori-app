## Demonstration journey

1. Register the reserved primary account in the app, make the real privacy choice, select Music from the actual catalogue and save. Reenter the editor, return Home and read real interest-based recommendations. Explain catalogue size honestly; the per-user selection cap is a different number.
2. Open a real recommendation and its detail. Show the missing-end label if applicable, open the exact validated source URL in the external browser and return. Open Browse, detail and Reload. Reload fetches the current first batch and may show the same activities; opening a website is not registration or Going.
3. Find the precreated secondary account and send a request. Sign out, sign in as secondary, accept and read its friend list. Sign out and return to primary; reread its friend list. Verify no prior-account content remains on screen after switching.
4. Save a short controlled Note, select its date in Footprint, open detail and return. Reopen to prove persistence. Use enough owned controlled records for pagination. On a controlled page failure retain the prior records and cursor, then retry that cursor after recovery.
5. Create or select a Persona and open AI. Send three ordinary messages, with the second and third referring to prior text. Leave and reenter to read persisted messages, then send again. The model receives at most the recent two eligible complete question/answer pairs; rejected/deleted/expired/unauthorized pairs do not backfill from older history. Full-input budget rejection has no silent truncation. A new conversation is available only after the prior operation result is determined.
6. Repeat the complete journey on the same APK/service; include logout/login, reentry and another actual use. Test cancellation last, then follow the original settlement result before any later action or cleanup.

## Recovery and stop rules

- Event source failure: show the safe error, never empty-success or stale validity. Use explicit Reload when the source is recovered. Disable source through its existing owned control if removal is requested; clear the same browse/recommend/detail projection.
- Definite local rejection or over-budget input: keep the draft, selection and focus; edit it or use New conversation only when permitted. Do not silently truncate or fabricate a reply.
- Unknown AI result: retain the original session/operation, use only its allowed original-operation recovery, and do not blind resend, retry, create a substitute operation, reset the service or force cleanup. An app terminal display does not prove remote generation stopped.
- Cold model: read-only checks establish the actual cold state. Existing explicitly authorized manual zero-content preload may restore current readiness only after settled/exclusive prerequisites; it is neither a successful user reply nor a permanent idle policy. Do not extend keep-alive or add a warm loop.
- Host/USB loss: keep current source/config/receipt and operation state; reestablish the existing private path only after verifying its original ownership and result. Do not clear either app or the whole Emulator environment.
- Final stop: close admission and drain; wait for confirmed original settlement before deleting exactly both controlled accounts and their owned records. Preserve unknown operations, foreign records, historical incident files and independent diagnostic retention. Exit/cleanup is not claimed until the service's exact result confirms it.
