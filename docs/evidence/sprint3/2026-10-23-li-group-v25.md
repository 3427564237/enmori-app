## Evidence and limits

The 54 composition/access cases have final passing evidence across the initial
48-case run and bounded corrections/additions; five device-language contract and
large-text checks passed separately. See the handoff for exact run accounting.
Source analysis covered 13 affected targets; the sole merge-created duplicate
import was removed and that file's recheck passed. All remaining targets had no
findings. Whitespace checks passed. Module source/fixture results are preserved
in their own notes rather than presented as one fresh full-suite run.

Two production defects were reproduced before correction: covered Events
callbacks duplicated detail routes, and authentication-invalid plan reads retained
old data. The fixes passed the actual group-route flow and all four explicit
access-loss variants. Old test assertions for AI route cancellation, general
Settings and Home personal shortcuts were corrected against the confirmed
22/23 October decisions. Synthetic AI requests now demonstrably survive normal
Home/Settings navigation without cancellation or another dispatch.

Seven module widget previews were collected under the local
`build/group-delivery-v25/previews/` directory. They contain synthetic data; App
module Settings captures precede injection of the real language control. Chinese
Social/App/Journal views were inspected, including 2× text. AI English plan
previews passed; its optional Chinese Persona heading had a fixture font defect
and is not counted as a visual pass. No production font workaround was added.

phone fonts/keyboard/TalkBack; real service save/readback or two-account revocation;
multi-round model/Persona verification; actual reboot/launcher recovery; iOS
compilation; shared-runtime switch; full UAT/Release/Done. The historical v24
intermittent initial AI Settings-read finding remains unresolved. Historical
unknown AI operations were not retried or replaced. No real data or running
