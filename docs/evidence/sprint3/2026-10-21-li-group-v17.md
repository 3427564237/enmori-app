## Weather diagnosis and limitation

The saved record was neither resubmitted nor enriched later. The current adapter follows the existing approved two-second total deadline and one request per logical creation (`docs/decisions/2026-09-02-production-weather-contract.md`, Q13-B). Missing weather can also result from validation and permission gates; the original request has no per-stage diagnostic, so its exact cause cannot be recovered from available evidence.

One separate bounded diagnostic used the deployed adapter code, Node 24.19.0, and only a 0.1-degree coarse grid. It made one provider call, received no headers before cancellation, returned no projection at approximately 2103 ms, and observed AbortError. This reproduces a provider-access timeout now; it does **not** prove the original save timed out. No precise point, note, identity or raw provider body was logged, and no business record was changed. The deadline was not silently extended, automatic retries were not added, and historical weather was not fabricated. Positive weather capture on a future new record remains to be observed when the provider responds within the approved budget.
